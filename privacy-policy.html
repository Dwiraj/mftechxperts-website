Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

MFTechXperts (“MFTechXperts”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects the privacy of the clients, partners, and visitors who use our website and services. This policy explains the categories of information we may receive, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include your name, business details, contact information, service requirements, case-related information, communications, and technical information such as browser or device details. We only request information that is relevant to the service or communication you choose to initiate.

2. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, provide and coordinate services, manage cases, communicate with you, improve our workflows, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or contractual requirements.

3. Client and case information

Our operating model is designed to minimize unnecessary retention of client information. Where access to third-party platforms, AMC/RTA systems, or client records is required for an agreed service, information is handled for the purpose of performing that service and according to the applicable engagement arrangements.

4. Security

We use operational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, including controlled access practices, isolated browser profiles, hardened browser workflows, and firewall protections. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-party services

Our work may involve third-party platforms, AMCs, RTAs, communication tools, or other systems selected by you or required for the service. Their own privacy terms may apply to information processed within those systems.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant service, relationship, legal requirement, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purpose. Specific retention arrangements may be defined in an engagement or service agreement.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about the information we hold in relation to your interaction with MFTechXperts, subject to applicable legal, contractual, and security limitations.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@mftechxperts.com or call +91 91069 37917.
