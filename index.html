Security

How we approach access and data handling.

We use operational safeguards intended to reduce unnecessary access and retention. Specific arrangements can depend on the agreed service scope.

01

Purpose-limited handling

Information is handled to provide the agreed service and according to applicable engagement arrangements.

02

Access practices

Access to platforms and records is used for the work agreed with each distributor.

03

Operational safeguards

Controlled access, separate browser profiles, hardened workflows, and firewall protections are used to reduce unauthorized access.

04

Retention arrangements

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary, subject to service, legal, and contractual requirements.
